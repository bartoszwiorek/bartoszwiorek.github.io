# Privacy, simply put.

This is the personal website of Bartosz Wiorek. For questions about your privacy, contact [bartoszwiorek@gmail.com](mailto:bartoszwiorek@gmail.com).

## Your choice

When consent management is enabled, Cookiebot lets you choose which optional services may run. You can revisit your choice using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Without consent management configured, optional analytics remain disabled.

## Website analytics

When configured, this website uses Ackee to understand visits. Tracking starts only after you allow the statistics category in Cookiebot. Detailed visitor tracking is disabled. Withdrawing statistics consent stops further visit-duration updates; it does not remove data already sent.

## External services

Cookiebot is loaded when configured to manage your preferences. Ackee connects to the website owner's configured analytics server only after statistics consent. These services receive network requests, which include your IP address. Hosting also processes requests to deliver this website.

LinkedIn opens an external website with its own privacy practices. Sending an email uses your chosen email provider.
